# Mechatronics I Final Project
(Please note the site is not final, and only the check-in will be done until the final submission where the whole site will be completed.)
## Project Description

Our original goal was to create a fan that cooled objects off when they are in certain range of an ultrasonic sensor. Our MVP was to have a fan turn on when they are within a certain range of an object, and our stretch goal was to have it on a servo and track an object or find the closest object to cool off. 


## Check In 1

I did some research on a a commercial product from Dreo that does what I am trying to do. They use a millimeter wave sensor plus motion detectors to track objects in the room point a fan at them. The Dreo smart fan "has a millimeter-wave radar sensor built in that tracks where people are in a room, and “adjusts its airflow towards the individual,” says Joshua Gunn, vice president of Dreo." I am trying to achieve this but with an ultrasonic sensor, which differs in it's capabilities, the main one being that an ultrasonic sensor is less accurate and is a much lower frequency, which results in a much lower sampling rate. This can cause the tracking to "lag" behind the moving object. 

I did meet my goal of getting the ultrasonic sensor up and running and being able to use an LED to show that something is within range of it. Since I was able to complete this jumping to turning on a motor will be very easy. So by the next check in I can hopefully show progress on one of my stretch goals. I do not need pivot as my MVP is very achievable and doable.
