# A8 – Gears

## Objective
The main objective of this assignment is to learn how to design using 3rd party models such as gears, along with creating assemblys.

## Calculation of Bearings
After solving for the shafts I then solved for the forces acting on the bearings.

![](a7_5.png) 

I again followed the same process as solving for each of the shafts, First drawing an FBD and then listing all knowns and unknowns.

![](a7_6.png) 

I then solved for the forces symbolically, then following up with the solution for the problem numerically.

![](a7_7.png) 
![](a7_8.png) 

After solving for all forces, pressures and velocities on the bearings, I decided the best material to have these bearings made up of is Bronze. I choose this due to our requirements falling comfortably within the ranges for Bronze.
After picking material I then solved for the minimum thickness needed for the bearings just to double check that the material I choose is up to the task.
